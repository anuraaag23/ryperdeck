## 💡 What is RyperDeck?

Traditional hardware macro controllers and stream decks cost upwards of **$150 to $250** and lock you into a rigid physical grid of 15 buttons.

**RyperDeck** turns any existing smartphone or tablet into a fluid, responsive, wireless Windows macro controller. Built with an ultra-tactile **Liquid Glass** aesthetic, zero-latency local UDP networking, and limitless custom pages, RyperDeck gives you workstation-level control without spending a single dollar on proprietary plastic hardware.

- ⚡ **Local UDP Protocol**: Commands trigger natively on Windows with sub-millisecond response (`< 1ms`).
- 🔒 **100% Private**: Zero cloud telemetry, no accounts required, no keystroke tracking. All data stays inside your local Wi-Fi network.
- 📑 **Infinite Workspaces**: Unlimited dynamic pages for Streaming (OBS), Gaming, Video Editing (Premiere, DaVinci), Audio Mixing, and Coding.
- 👨‍💻 **Built by One Person**: Designed and crafted by [Anurag](https://github.com/anuraaag23) (Age 20) for the global creator community.

## 🚀 How It Works

Getting started takes less than 60 seconds:

1. **Install the Windows Companion**: Run the lightweight Windows companion app on your PC (runs silently in your system tray using under 15MB RAM).
2. **Open the Mobile App**: Launch RyperDeck on your Android phone or tablet connected to the same Wi-Fi router.
3. **Control Instantly**: Devices automatically discover each other over UDP broadcast. Tap any tile to trigger macros, audio tweaks, and app launches instantly.

## 💻 System Requirements

- **PC**: Windows 11 or Windows 10 (64-bit)
- **Mobile**: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher (phones & tablets)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Is RyperDeck truly 100% free?</strong></summary>
Yes. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no advertisements, and no artificial page limits. All core macro features and .json export/import capabilities are free forever.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does RyperDeck require an active internet connection?</strong></summary>
No. All communication between your Windows PC and your phone happens locally over your Wi-Fi router via UDP multipeer packets. Your keystrokes never leave your home network.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I leave an old tablet plugged in next to my PC monitor?</strong></summary>
Yes! That is how most Windows power users run RyperDeck. Leave an old tablet or phone on a stand beside your keyboard, and it will automatically reconnect whenever your PC boots.
</details>
